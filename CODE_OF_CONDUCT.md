# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Pasted should be a welcoming place to build, test, document, and discuss software. Participants are expected to treat one another with respect regardless of experience, identity, background, ability, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and kind.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, and reasonable boundaries.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful impact.
- Accept correction gracefully and help repair mistakes.
- Keep public technical channels useful to the broader community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized attention or unwelcome personal contact;
- deliberate humiliation, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- pressuring anyone to reveal clipboard data, credentials, identity, or private communications;
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in Pasted repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, release channels, community spaces, and private interactions undertaken as a project representative.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not litigate sensitive conduct reports in a public issue. Submit a confidential report through [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/getpasted/pasted/security/advisories/new) and clearly label it as a **Code of Conduct report**, not a vulnerability.

Project maintainers will review reports as privately and promptly as practical. Consequences may include a private warning, removal of content, temporary participation limits, or permanent removal from project spaces. Maintainers will avoid conflicts of interest and share report details only as needed to investigate and act.

False reports made in bad faith may themselves violate this policy. Disagreement, critical review, and respectfully declining a proposal do not.
